## What HundredX sells

- Double-blind, ethically sourced consumer feedback collected through HundredX Causes programs,
  which fund nonprofits for every qualifying response.
- Coverage described by the company as 80+ industries and roughly two-thirds of the US economy.
- The HundredX GO Score (Growth Outlook), positioned as a 6-12 month leading indicator of
  revenue growth and market performance.
- Delivery is sales-led: a customer portal, negotiated data feeds, and partner alliances
  (Deloitte, Goldman Sachs). Not an API product.

## MCP

HundredX runs two publicly reachable MCP servers, "HX BigQuery MCP Server" 2.0.0 and
"Jupyter MCP Server" 2.0.0, both OAuth 2.1 with Google-federated identity. THEY ARE INTERNAL
ANALYST TOOLING over HundredX's own warehouse and notebooks (scopes bigquery.readonly,
cloud-platform), not a customer-facing agent product, and HundredX publishes no documentation
for them. An anonymous `tools/list` returns 401 invalid_token, so no tool schemas are known.
